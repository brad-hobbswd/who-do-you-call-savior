About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

Christian author, teacher, and follower of Jesus Christ. I write to share the truth of Scripture, encourage believers, and point readers toward the hope found in Him.

Welcome

It's a Pleasure to Meet You

My name is Bradley Hobbs. Before anything else, I am a follower of Jesus Christ. My faith is the foundation of my life and the reason I write. I want the words on these pages to reflect the truth of Scripture and encourage people to know the Lord more deeply.

My journey has included seasons of joy, growth, uncertainty, and testing. Those experiences have taught me to depend on the grace of God, hold firmly to His promises, and remember that His love remains faithful through every season.

I consider writing a privilege and a responsibility. I do not want to publish books simply to add more words to the world. I want to share biblical truth in a way that encourages people to open their Bibles, consider what God has said, and follow Jesus with a deeper and more faithful commitment.

My Story

Faith Through Every Season

Life does not always unfold as we expect. There are seasons when the way forward seems clear and others when faith is tested by uncertainty, disappointment, or difficulty. Through each season, I have been reminded that God's faithfulness does not depend on my circumstances. His Word remains true, His grace is sufficient, and His mercy reaches farther than we can imagine.

These lessons have shaped the way I see people and the way I approach ministry. I know that many carry questions, private struggles, and burdens that are not always visible. My hope is to offer writing that takes those realities seriously while directing readers toward the lasting hope and truth found in Jesus Christ.

My Calling

A Calling to Teach and Encourage

Teaching, ministry, and writing give me different opportunities to serve, but each carries the same responsibility: to encourage others and remain faithful to the truth of God's Word. I value the opportunity to teach, help people engage with Scripture, and share biblical principles in ways they can carry into everyday life.

I believe the message of Jesus is not meant to remain on a page or inside a church building. It should shape how we live, how we treat others, and how we respond when life becomes difficult. My prayer is that my work points beyond me and helps people see Christ more clearly.

Beyond the Books

The People and Purpose That Keep Me Grounded

Writing and teaching are meaningful parts of my life, but they do not define all of who I am. I am grateful for the opportunity to serve others, invest in the people around me, and continue learning what it means to follow Christ faithfully.

Family is among God's greatest gifts. The people closest to me provide love, encouragement, and perspective, reminding me that faith is lived out in the everyday moments of life as much as in the work we share with others.

My desire is to keep Jesus Christ at the center of my life, my family, and every responsibility He places before me.
