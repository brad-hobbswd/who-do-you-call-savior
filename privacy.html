Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit Who Do You Call Savior?

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the website Who Do You Call Savior?, created by Bradley Hobbs, handles information associated with your visit. The website shares information about the book, Christian teaching resources, and related material.

2. Information You Provide

You may choose to contact the author through contact information or communication options that are made available on the website. Information you voluntarily provide in a direct communication may include your name, email address, and the contents of your message. Please do not send sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information through a general inquiry.

3. Website Forms

A form displayed on this website should not be assumed to transmit a message unless the page clearly confirms that a working submission service is connected. Do not rely on a form to deliver urgent or important communication.

4. Technical Information and Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and related infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and access times as part of delivering and securing the website. Such processing is governed by the provider's own policies and practices.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or operate its own analytics program through this policy. Third-party services or embedded content, if added, may use their own cookies or similar technologies and are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Sharing and Selling Information

Bradley Hobbs does not sell personal information submitted directly to him through voluntary communication. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users, or legal rights.

7. External Links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their content, security, and privacy practices are outside the control of this website. Review their policies before providing information.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of websites and online communication.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be revised as the website or its features change. The effective date above indicates when this version was published.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact options provided on the Contact page.
