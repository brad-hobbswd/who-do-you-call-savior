# Who Do You Call Savior?
## Every Story Is Different. Every Soul Needs the Same Savior.

**Who Do You Call Savior?** by Bradley Hobbs is a Scripture-centered Christian book and online study about salvation, grace, repentance, forgiveness, redemption, faith, and the new life available through Jesus Christ. The site brings together the book, author information, study resources, prayer resources, Bible study topics, and related titles in the Question Series.

## Read Online

**Website:** https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-do-you-call-savior/

Start with the [Introduction](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-do-you-call-savior/contents/introduction/), browse the [Table of Contents](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-do-you-call-savior/contents/), or visit the [Resources Library](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-do-you-call-savior/resources/).

## About the Book

The central message of this project is that every person needs a Savior and that salvation, hope, forgiveness, and redemption are found in Jesus Christ. The book invites readers to look beyond labels and past failures, examine Scripture, and respond personally to the gospel.

> “Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.”
>
> **Acts 4:12**

## The Question Series

This book is part of a wider collection that includes:

- [Who Made You Judge?](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-made-you-judge/)
- [Who Gave You Authority?](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-gave-you-authority/)
- **Who Do You Call Savior?**

Together, the books encourage readers to examine Scripture, question assumptions, and return to the truth of God's Word.
